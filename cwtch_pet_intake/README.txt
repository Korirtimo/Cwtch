Cwtch Rescue intake photos: practice data for Computational AI
==============================================================

images/                 284 intake photos (JPG and PNG)
intake_metadata.csv     one row per file: file_name, animal_id, intake_date, device,
                        species_entered, breed_entered

Cwtch Rescue is fictional. The photos are a subset of the Oxford-IIIT Pet Dataset:
Parkhi, O.M., Vedaldi, A., Zisserman, A. and Jawahar, C.V. (2012) 'Cats and dogs',
IEEE Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition.
https://www.robots.ox.ac.uk/~vgg/data/pets/
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-SA 4.0).

Changes made for teaching: images were cropped, rotated, resized, converted between
colour modes, darkened, given synthetic noise, duplicated, truncated or blanked, and
the metadata was invented. This adapted set is shared under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 licence.
